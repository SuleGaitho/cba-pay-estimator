CBA 2025–2029 pay estimator

Estimate your new salary, back pay and take-home under the Return to Work Formula of 30 October 2026.

How to use V1.0
  1. Pick your staff group: Academic (UASU) or Administrative (KUSU).
  2. Choose your grade and notch exactly as on your July 2025 payslip.
  3. Enter your allowances: current commuter allowance, house allowance and pension percentage. Tick or untick NSSF to match your payslip.
  4. Set your increment month (the month your notch normally moves up).
  5. Open More options if you were promoted, want the full commuter amount from July 2025, or need to set the month book and ICT allowance is paid (UASU).
  6. Read your results: back pay, new monthly gross and take-home, the two back pay payments, and extra pay per financial year.
  7. Check yourself: type your July 2025 basic in "Basic on July 2025 payslip". A warning appears if it does not match your grade and notch.
  8. Share, print or send feedback using the buttons at the bottom of the page.

Results update instantly as you change any field. Nothing you enter leaves your device.
