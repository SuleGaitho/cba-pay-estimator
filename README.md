# CBA 2025-2029 Pay Estimator (V1.0)

A free web tool that helps Kenyan public university staff (UASU academic and
KUSU administrative) estimate their new salary, back pay and take-home pay
under the 2025-2029 Collective Bargaining Agreement.

**Live tool:** https://SuleGaitho.github.io/cba-pay-estimator/

## How to use

1. Choose your staff group: Academic (UASU) or Administrative (KUSU).
2. Select your grade and notch as on your July 2025 payslip.
3. Enter commuter allowance, house allowance and pension percentage. Set NSSF to match your payslip.
4. Choose your increment month.
5. Open "More options" for promotions, the full commuter amount, or book and ICT allowance month.
6. Read your results. Use "Basic on July 2025 payslip" to check your grade and notch.
7. Share, print or send feedback from the buttons at the bottom.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere.

## Disclaimer

Unofficial estimate for planning only. Not payroll advice and not affiliated
with IPUCCF, FKE, UASU, KUSU or any university. Confirm final figures with your
payroll office.
